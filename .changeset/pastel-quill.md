Sequill moves to the Pastel Quill design system. The dark theme is a neutral charcoal with no blue cast, with the working sheet a step darker than its frame; corners, borders and shadows are softer and thinner; and buttons, inputs and menus share one set of sizes. The schema designer uses a compact density and the editor follows the density you choose. The design is written up in DESIGN.md, with previews of the landing page, the editor and every component at `/test`.
